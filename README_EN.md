# Jurilu AI

## An AI video production platform for professional short drama and animated comic teams

Jurilu AI is a web-based production platform for AI short dramas, animated comics, and related video content. It connects script analysis, character and scene assets, storyboarding, shot generation, automated review, regeneration of unsuccessful shots, and editing-oriented delivery in one workflow.

Official website: [https://video.jurilu.com](https://video.jurilu.com)

中文说明：[README.md](README.md)

## What Jurilu AI does

Producing a complete AI drama may involve hundreds of shots. Different shots require different levels of motion, camera work, character interaction, visual consistency, and model capability. Teams otherwise spend substantial time selecting models, testing generations, reviewing outputs, and repeating failed shots.

Jurilu AI organizes this work as a production system. Its model-routing mechanism can match shots with suitable models according to shot difficulty and production requirements. Automated stages retain checkpoints where creators can review, modify, and approve the result.

## Main capabilities

- Script-to-drama workflow for full scripts, episodes, assets, storyboards, and generated shots.
- Model routing based on shot difficulty and production requirements.
- One-click production designed for complete episodes and multi-shot projects.
- Automated shot review and regeneration of outputs that do not meet requirements.
- Reusable team assets, including characters, scenes, visual styles, and project materials.
- Human control at key stages such as script analysis, asset design, storyboarding, generation, review, and editing.
- Three production approaches: visual-quality focused, balanced, and efficiency focused.

## Typical use cases

- AI live-action short dramas
- AI animated comics and motion comics
- Script and fiction adaptation
- Advertising and branded short-form video
- Creative video and film-style reinterpretation
- Scalable content production for studios and creator teams
- Educational stories, explainers, and knowledge videos
